GaadiBook — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Automotive Two-Wheeler Workshop Operating System

1. Overview & Offline-First Architecture

GaadiBook ("we", "our", or "app") is developed to empower independent two-wheeler motorcycle and scooter workshops across India. GaadiBook is built with an offline-first local database (Drift SQLite). Your workshop invoices, job cards, customer records, and daily cash transactions reside securely on your physical device.

2. Data We Collect & How We Use It

  • Account & Contact Info: Mobile phone number and optional Google Profile credentials (email and display name) for authentication, workshop owner identification, and optional multi-device cloud synchronization.
  • Workshop Records: Workshop name, physical address, vehicle registration numbers, job cards, parts/labor tallies, and cashbook records created by your garage.
  • Device Permissions:
    • Camera: Used on-device for Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to detect Indian vehicle registration plates and capture optional scratch/damage vehicle intake photos.
    • Microphone: Used exclusively for on-device voice dictation to convert mechanic spoken speech into job complaint chips. Audio recordings are not permanently stored or sold.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We never sell, lease, or monetize your workshop records, financial balances, or customer contacts to third-party advertisers. All optional cloud synchronizations use industry-standard HTTPS encryption and PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) to ensure strict workshop isolation.

4. User Account & Data Deletion Policy

In strict compliance with Google Play Data Safety and User Data policies, all users have the right to permanently delete their account and associated workshop data at any time.

🗑️ Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your account and data through either of the following two options:

Option A — In-App Instant Wipe:

Open the GaadiBook app on your phone → Tap Settings → Select Account & Workshop Profile → Tap Delete Account & Data. This instantly purges all local SQLite records, active login tokens, and staff memberships from your physical device.

Option B — Web Request (No app reinstallation needed):

If you have uninstalled the app or lost your mobile device, you can request account and cloud data deletion by sending an email with your registered mobile number:

Submit Deletion Request via Email

What data is permanently deleted?

  • Your user profile, authentication ID, and Google account linkage.
  • Your workshop membership, staff assignments, and permissions.
  • All job cards, customer directory profiles, damage photos, and invoices synced to our cloud servers.

Processing Timeline:

Upon receiving an email deletion request, your account and all associated cloud records are permanently purged from all production databases within 30 days.
